\begin{abstract}
This working draft synthesizes recent literature on Long Chain-of-Thought (Long-CoT) reasoning in large language models. We structure 24 representative studies across four technical research roles: A1 (Training and Data Strategies), A2 (Inference and Test-Time Control), A3 (Analysis and Mechanistic Understanding), and A4 (Evaluation Frameworks and Benchmarks). In addition, we review nine prior survey taxonomies, examining their organizing principles, scoping frameworks, and comparative perspectives. The collection prioritizes direct Long-CoT studies while marking adjacent and foundational evidence.
\end{abstract}
