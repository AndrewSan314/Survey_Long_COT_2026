\begin{abstract}
This working draft synthesizes recent literature on Long Chain-of-Thought (Long-CoT) reasoning in large language models. We organize 24 representative studies into 15 methodological subgroups under four research roles: A1 (Training/Data), A2 (Inference/Control), A3 (Analysis), and A4 (Evaluation/Benchmark). We compare nine prior survey taxonomies by scope and organizing unit, and explain how they relate to research-role assignments. Beyond reporting methods and outcomes, the extended synthesis examines training and inference trade-offs, levels of analytical evidence, evaluation units, sampling, and full-system compute accounting. Eight proposed research questions connect these roles through concrete experimental designs. The collection prioritizes direct Long-CoT studies while marking adjacent and foundational evidence; it is selective, and reported experiments have not been independently reproduced.
\end{abstract}
